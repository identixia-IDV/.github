# Community code of conduct

Treat everyone participating in Identixia repositories with respect. Welcome questions, give constructive feedback, and focus technical disagreements on the work.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, spam, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. Respect the privacy of people whose identity or biometric data may be involved in an integration; use synthetic examples in public discussions.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to [contact@identixia.com](mailto:contact@identixia.com), including links and context where possible. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, close disruptive conversations, or restrict participation to protect the community.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community interactions in Identixia-managed repositories.
